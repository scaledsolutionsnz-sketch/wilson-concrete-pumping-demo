About us

The crew that gets the mix where it needs to go

Wilson Concrete Pumping is a local, owner-operated concrete pumping outfit. We work alongside builders, concrete placers and homeowners to get every pour placed accurately, safely and without the backbreaking wheelbarrow work.

Line pump or boom, straightforward slab or awkward back-section, we bring the right gear and an operator who knows how to run it. Book the pump, we'll match the timing to your ready-mix truck and keep the pour moving.

  • Locally owned and operated
  • Clear, up-front pricing before we start
  • Timed to your ready-mix so nothing sits waiting
