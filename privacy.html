Privacy Policy

How Wilson Concrete Pumping collects and looks after your information. Last updated August 2026.

This is placeholder wording provided for the demo website. Final privacy wording should be reviewed to reflect how Wilson Concrete Pumping actually handles customer information.

1. Overview

Wilson Concrete Pumping ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or book our services, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Your name and contact details (phone, email) when you call or email us for a quote.
  • Job details such as the site address, access, pour date and volume of concrete.
  • Any information you choose to share when arranging or discussing a booking.

3. How we use it

We use your information to quote, schedule and carry out your concrete pumping job, to keep in touch about your booking, and to send invoices or follow up on work completed.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We only share it where needed to deliver your job — for example coordinating timing with your concrete supplier — or where required by law.

5. Storage & security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the job, our records and legal obligations, and take reasonable steps to protect it from loss or misuse.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to stop contacting you. Just get in touch using the details below.
